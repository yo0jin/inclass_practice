Building Better Study Habits - 6 Target Expressions Activity

1. keep track of
기록하다 / 파악하다
I keep track of my daily study time.
2. take a break
휴식을 취하다
Let's take a break and get some fresh air.
3. stay away from
~을 멀리하다
Try to stay away from your smartphone while studying.
4. get enough sleep
충분한 수면을 취하다
You need to get enough sleep to stay healthy.
5. deal with
~을 다루다 / 해소하다
A short walk helps me deal with stress.
6. stick to
~을 지키다 / 고수하다
It is good to stick to your new routine.
